The idea

With an analytical mind, I've always seen the world as a bunch of equations that are solvable. But over the last few years, I've come to recognise that life doesn't offer linear — or even deterministic — equations. Problems are abstract, and they get amplified when emotions come into play. For a person like me, that's unsettling.

And yet, so many of the debates we hear and engage with every day can actually be contextualised with the right numbers. Plot.me.twist is my attempt to plot the everyday questions we all have — with data — and give readers the context to steer any conversation in a more structured direction.
